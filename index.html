HERITAGE & CRAFT MANIFESTO

The Senator Suit as Modern Sovereign Armor

Born along the Niger Delta and elevated across Lagos high society, the Senator wear is the undisputed modern sartorial signature of the African gentleman. It replaces Western conventions with regal band collars, concealed plackets, and geometric chest embroidery inspired by ancestral motifs.

At Moda Couture, every piece begins with hand-selected Super 140s Italian wool, Swiss voile cotton, or handwoven Aso-Oke cloth. We cut each garment by hand in our Lagos workshop, ensuring sharp shoulder balance, smooth armhole movement, and trousers that drape without break onto handcrafted leather footwear.
